## العربية

**الاسم:** إتمام

**الوصف:**

إتمام يقترح عليك الكلمات أثناء الكتابة بلوحة المفاتيح في أي برنامج على Windows، بالعربية والإنجليزية ولغات أخرى، مثل اقتراحات الهاتف: شريط صغير فوق المؤشر فيه أقرب الكلمات لما بدأت كتابته، تختار منها بضغطة Tab.

يعمل في المفكرة وWord والمتصفحات ومعظم البرامج، من قاموس عربي فيه نحو 880 ألف كلمة وقاموس إنجليزي بنحو 600 ألف، ويتعلّم كلماتك أنت فيقدّم ما تستعمله أكثر، وبأي لغة أبجدية أخرى تفعّلها.

خصوصيتك أولًا. لا يرسل إتمام أي بيانات: لا إحصاءات ولا تقارير أعطال. الاتصال التلقائي الوحيد سؤال متجر Microsoft عن التحديثات، ويمكن إيقافه. ولا يحفظ نصوصك: يحفظ الكلمات والعبارات القصيرة التي تكررها مع عدد مرات استعمالها فقط، على جهازك. ولا يعمل في حقول كلمات المرور ولا في التطبيقات التي تستثنيها. والشيفرة المصدرية مفتوحة للمراجعة على GitHub.

## English

**Name:** Itmam

**Description:**

Itmam suggests words as you type on your keyboard, in any Windows app, in Arabic, English and other languages, like the suggestions on a phone: a small bar above the caret shows the closest words to what you started typing, and Tab inserts the one you want.

It works in Notepad, Word, browsers and most apps, with an Arabic dictionary of about 880,000 words and an English one of about 600,000, and learns your own words so the ones you use most come first, in any other alphabetic language you turn on too.

It also saves you repeated typing: it completes well-known phrases, turns your shortcuts into full text, and inserts long replies of several lines, such as support answers, with one shortcut.

Your privacy first. Itmam sends no data: no statistics, no crash reports. Its only automatic connection is asking Microsoft Store about updates, and it can be turned off. Your text is not saved: only the words and short phrases you repeat, with how many times you used them, on your PC. It does nothing in password fields or in apps you exclude. The source code is open for review on GitHub.

**Features** (up to 20):
- Instant suggestions as you type, in any app
- A dictionary of about 880,000 words from everyday Arabic and Arabic Wikipedia, or the lighter basic one
- Several languages: follows your keyboard, with a full English dictionary, and learns any other alphabetic language you turn on
- Learns your words and puts the ones you use most first
- Understands writing without hamza: «احمد» finds «أحمد»
- Fixes common mistakes: «لاكن» → «لكن», «انشاءالله» → «إن شاء الله»
- Completes phrases: «بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم», «جزاك الله خيرا» and about 250 more
- Learns the phrases you repeat; "My phrases" for your own
- Text shortcuts: type «ع1» and it offers «السلام عليكم ورحمة الله وبركاته»
- Long texts of several lines with variables (customer name, today's date), such as support replies, chosen with a shortcut in any app
- Continues a word from the middle after a click or Backspace
- Tab to insert, Ctrl + a number or the arrow keys for the others; Backspace right after undoes it
- Right-click a suggestion: delete it, block the word, or add the phrase to your phrases
- A Windows 11 style bar, light or dark, in the colors you choose
- Configurable shortcuts; Ctrl+Alt turns it off and on
- "My words": add, edit, block, import and export
- Silent in password fields and full-screen games; turn it off in any app with one click
- Full backup in one file, and statistics of what it typed for you
- No ads, no data collection; updates only through Microsoft Store
- Arabic and English interface; open source (MIT)

## Notes for certification

Paste into Partner Center → Submission options → Notes for certification.

> **What Itmam does.** Itmam is a word-prediction app for the physical keyboard (Arabic and English dictionaries; other
> alphabetic languages learn from the user's typing once turned on). While the user types with a keyboard layout of a
> language turned on, it shows a small suggestion bar above the caret; Tab (or a click) inserts the chosen word.
> It runs in the notification area (tray); click the tray icon (either button) and choose Settings.
>
> **How to test.** Add an Arabic keyboard layout (Settings → Time & language → Language & region → Arabic), switch to
> it, open Notepad and type «مرح»: a bar with word suggestions appears above the caret, and Tab inserts the first
> one. With an English layout, type "hel": the bar offers "help", "hello" … Settings → Languages lists the keyboard
> languages and turns them on or off.
>
> **Why runFullTrust and a global keyboard hook.** Word prediction must see the keys typed in other apps, which is only
> possible with a low-level keyboard hook (`SetWindowsHookEx(WH_KEYBOARD_LL)`) in a full-trust desktop process;
> inserting the chosen word uses `SendInput`; the caret position and the word around it are read with UI Automation
> (at most 49 characters before the caret and 30 after it, never from password fields or read-only text; only the
> current word is kept, in memory).
> - Keys are tracked only while the foreground window uses a layout of a language turned on (Arabic and English by
>   default); never with input method editors (Chinese, Japanese, Korean).
> - Nothing typed is logged or sent anywhere; the app has no network capability. Its only automatic online request is
>   the update check through `Windows.Services.Store.StoreContext` (Store service; can be turned off in Settings →
>   Updates). The "Rate and review" button (Updates and About pages) calls `RequestRateAndReviewAppAsync` only when
>   pressed.
> - Only word → use count / last use is stored, locally (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Itmam\user.db`), plus 2–5-word sequences
>   with their counts to learn repeated phrases (can be turned off; sequences not repeated are deleted after 30 days).
> - Long texts (replies the user saved, list opened with Ctrl+Shift+Space) are inserted by pasting: the user's
>   clipboard is saved and restored right after, and the temporary text is excluded from clipboard history and the
>   cloud clipboard.
> - It is silent in password fields (ES_PASSWORD, UI Automation IsPassword), elevated apps, excluded apps and
>   full-screen games.
> - Full details: https://nasmn77.github.io/Itmam/en/privacy.html and https://github.com/nasmn77/Itmam (source code and docs/ARCHITECTURE.md are public).
>
> **Windows App Certification Kit.** "Blocked executables" lists strings such as `cmd` inside the self-contained .NET 10
> runtime files and the compressed dictionary data, and `Process.Start` / `ShellExecute`, which Itmam uses only to
> open https links in the user's browser (About page), Itmam's own Store page when an update is available, its review
> page if the Store's rating window cannot open, and the
> Windows typing settings page (`ms-settings:typing`, Settings → Languages, to turn off Windows' own suggestion bar). Itmam
> never launches other programs. The High-DPI warning: WinForms sets PerMonitorV2 at start-up
> (`ApplicationHighDpiMode`), not in the manifest.
