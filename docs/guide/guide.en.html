1. What Itmam is

Itmam suggests words while you type on your keyboard, in Arabic, English and other languages, the way a phone does: a small bar appears above the caret with the closest words to what you started typing, and one key inserts the one you want.

  • In every app: Notepad, Word, browsers, email and most apps, with no setup.
  • A large dictionary: about 880,000 words from everyday Arabic and Arabic Wikipedia, and an English dictionary of about 600,000 words.
  • Several languages: it follows your keyboard layout and suggests in the language you are typing, and learns the words of any other alphabetic language you turn on (chapter 10).
  • Learns from you: the words you use become suggestions, and the ones you use most come first.
  • Saves repeated typing: completes well-known phrases, turns your shortcuts into full text, and inserts long replies of several lines.
  • Private: it sends no data and does not save your text.

2. Before you start

Keyboard layouts

Itmam suggests in the language of the layout you type with: Arabic and English are on from the start, and any other language can be turned on in Settings → "Languages". To add a layout: Windows Settings → Time & language → Language & region → Add a language. Switch layouts with Alt + Shift or Win + Space.

Where is Itmam?

Itmam opens no window: it runs in the notification area next to the clock, and its icon is a bar with the letter «ت». When it starts, an "Itmam is running" notification appears. If you cannot see the icon, click the ^ arrow next to the clock.

Click the icon with either mouse button to open its menu (chapter 8).

Start with Windows

Itmam can start automatically with Windows: Settings → General → "Start with Windows".

3. Typing with suggestions

Type the first two letters of a word, and the bar appears above the caret.

أهلًا و مرح

Choosing a suggestion

  • Tab inserts the first suggestion, the one highlighted in the selection color.
  • Ctrl + 2 or 3… inserts a suggestion by its number. Numbers can be shown on the bar (Appearance).
  • With the arrow keys: ↑ moves into the bar, ← → choose, Enter inserts, Esc or ↓ leaves.
  • Or click the suggestion.
  • Esc hides the bar until you type the next letter.

After inserting, Itmam adds a space (can be turned off in General), but not when a space or punctuation already follows the caret.

Picked the wrong one?

Press Backspace right after inserting, and what you typed comes back.

It understands how you type

  • Without hamza: «احمد» finds «أحمد», and «ان» offers «أن», «إن» and «أنا».
  • With attached letters: the article and attached conjunctions and prepositions: «والمد» finds «والمدرسة».
  • Common mistakes: «لاكن» offers «لكن» first, and «انشاءالله» offers «إن شاء الله». Nothing is replaced automatically: you choose.
  • From the middle of a word: go back into a word with a click or Backspace, and Itmam completes it from the caret.

No suggestions appear while text is selected, in password fields, in excluded apps, or in full-screen games.

4. Your call from the bar: right-click

Right-click any suggestion, and buttons appear above it:

  • Delete: for a word Itmam learned from you: it is forgotten (and learned again if you use it). For a phrase: removed from your phrases, or hidden if it is built in.
  • Block: for a word you never want: it is never suggested, even if it is in the dictionary. Unblock it in "My words".
  • Add to My phrases: for a phrase, to keep it.

From the keyboard: while choosing with the arrows, Ctrl + Delete forgets the selected learned word.

5. Phrases

Itmam knows about 250 common phrases: greetings, remembrance, prayers, occasions and formal letters. Type the start of one and it offers the rest:

  • After «بسم » it offers «الله الرحمن الرحيم», and Tab inserts it whole. Or keep going word by word.
  • Your own: your signature or your organization's name, added in "My phrases", are suggested first.
  • Learned phrases: words you type in a row twice (2 to 5 words) are learned as a phrase. Sequences not repeated are deleted after 30 days. This can be turned off.

6. Text shortcuts

A short code you type: the bar offers its text first, and Tab puts it in place of the code. For example, type «ع1» and it offers «السلام عليكم ورحمة الله وبركاته».

A code starts with a letter of any language («ع1» or «brb») and may be followed by digits. Add yours in Settings → "Text shortcuts". A code is never replaced automatically: «ع1» stays as it is unless you press Tab.

7. Long texts

Replies of several lines you write often, such as support answers: the greeting, asking for the order number, the password reset steps, closing the ticket.

  1. Save each reply once in Settings → "Long texts" → "Add": a name you know it by, and a text of up to 4,000 characters over several lines.
  2. In any app, press Ctrl + Shift + Space: a list of your replies opens. To add a new reply without opening Settings, press "Add a text…" in the list itself: the new reply is then selected in it.
  3. Type part of a name or of the text to filter it, and move with the arrow keys while you see the reply's text below the list.
  4. Press Enter or "Insert": the reply is pasted at the caret with its lines as you wrote them. Esc or "Cancel" closes the list.

Editing a reply before inserting it

The reply's text below the list can be edited: click in it and change what you like. Enter there starts a new line and Ctrl + Enter inserts. When you insert after an edit, Itmam asks: "Yes" saves the edit in the saved text, then inserts it; "No" inserts the edited text this time only. Choosing another reply or closing the list drops the edit.

The reply is inserted through the clipboard; Itmam then puts back what was on your clipboard, and the reply stays out of clipboard history.

For support teams

Export the approved replies to a text file and import it on your colleagues' PCs, so everyone sends the same wording. Importing adds to what each person has and removes nothing.

Long texts: variables

Write the reply once and leave places for what changes each time: the customer's name, the order number, today's date.

  • Asked for {customer name}: in braces; Itmam asks for its value when you insert the reply. If it appears more than once, you type it once.
  • Automatic {{date}}: in double braces; Itmam writes it by itself. There are four: the date, the Hijri date (Umm al-Qura calendar), the day and the time. They are written in Arabic in an Arabic reply and in English in an English one.

Inserting a variable

In the long text window, press "Insert variable…", then choose: "Asked for" and type its name, or "Automatic" and pick it from the list. Itmam types the braces for you. You can also type a variable asked for yourself; automatic ones are picked from the list, and any other name in double braces is refused when saving.

Filling in the variables

Choose the reply in the long texts list and press Enter: a field appears for each variable asked for, and the reply below follows what you type. Tab moves between fields, Enter in the last one or "Insert" inserts the reply, and Esc or "Cancel" goes back to the list. If a field is left empty, Itmam asks before inserting. A reply without variables is inserted at once, as before.

You can also edit the filled reply itself, for this time only: the fields are then locked so they do not overwrite your edit, and the reply is inserted as you edited it.

The values you type are not saved; they are forgotten after inserting.

9. Settings: General

Open Settings from the tray menu. The pages are listed on the left, and every change applies at once.

  • User guide: opens this guide.
  • Start with Windows, and Notify when Itmam is turned off or on.
  • Suggestions: how many the bar shows (1 to 5), letters before they appear, and a space after accepting.
  • Learning: learn new words or not, and how many uses before a new word is suggested.

General: backup, statistics

  • Full backup: one file (.itmam) with your words, phrases, text shortcuts, long texts, settings and statistics, to move Itmam to another PC. "Restore" replaces everything on this PC with the file's content, after a confirmation.
  • Statistics: how many words and phrases Itmam completed and how many characters it typed for you. Only these two numbers are kept.

10. Languages

Itmam follows your keyboard layout and suggests in the language you are typing. The page has a card for each keyboard language on your PC, with what Itmam does in it and a switch to turn it on or off.

  • Interface language: Arabic or English, at the top of the page. On first run it is Arabic if Windows is displayed in Arabic, English otherwise.
  • Full dictionary: Arabic and English: dictionary words, common phrases and common mistakes. In English a suggestion follows the case you type («Lon» → «London»), and knows don't and I'm.
  • No dictionary: any other alphabetic language (Hebrew, French, Russian …): it suggests the words and phrases you type in it, My phrases and your text shortcuts. Off until you turn it on.
  • Silent: languages typed with an input method (Chinese, Japanese, Korean …) or without spaces between words.
  • Memory: Itmam loads only the dictionaries of the languages that are on. Turning a language off frees its dictionary at once, and turning it on loads it again. Your learned words in it are kept.
  • Arabic dictionary: "Wikipedia" (the default), about 880,000 words: the basic dictionary plus the vocabulary of Arabic Wikipedia for formal writing and terms. "Basic": more than 500,000 words, using less memory.
  • English dictionary: "Wikipedia" (the default), about 600,000 words: adds names and terms in their right case («Microsoft»). "Basic": about 300,000 words, using less memory.
  • Two suggestion bars? If Windows shows its own bar too, turn off "Show text suggestions when typing on the physical keyboard"; "Open" takes you to that setting.

11. Appearance

  • Theme: System, Light or Dark, for the bar and the settings window.
  • Colors: bar, selection and text. "Automatic" picks the most readable color, and a warning appears if the text gets hard to read.
  • Bar: opacity, text size, highlighting the first suggestion, and showing suggestion numbers.
  • A live preview at the top; "Reset to the Windows look" resets everything.

12. Keyboard shortcuts

Click a shortcut, then press the new keys; click elsewhere to cancel. Itmam refuses shortcuts that type text or clash with common Windows shortcuts, and says why.

  • While the bar is shown: insert the first suggestion (Tab), insert by number (Ctrl or Alt with the number), hide (Esc), and choosing with the arrow keys.
  • Everywhere: turn Itmam on or off (Ctrl + Alt), and open the long texts list (Ctrl + Shift + Space).

13. Excluded apps

Itmam stays completely silent in these apps: no suggestions, no learning, nothing read.

  • Add an app: from the open apps, or "Browse…" for its program file.
  • The default list holds remote desktop, virtual machines, terminals and game launchers.
  • Quickest: "Turn off Itmam in …" from the tray menu while you are in the app.

14. My words

The words Itmam learned from you. Only the word, how many times you used it and when are kept, on this PC only.

  • Learn new words: when off, nothing new is learned, and your words are still suggested.
  • Add: a word suggested at once. Block: a word never suggested, even if it is in the dictionary.
  • Language: when you have words in more than one language, choose the list's language; everything you do on the page applies to it.
  • The list sorts by most used, recent or A–Z, with search. Click a word to edit, block or delete it.
  • Import: choose any text file (.txt), such as an article or old messages: Itmam takes the words of the chosen language from it, shows how many before adding them, and suggests them at once. The text itself is not kept. A Word document can be saved as "Plain text" first. A file exported from here imports too.
  • Export: a text file of your words, to back them up or move them. "Delete all my words" cannot be undone.

15. My phrases and text shortcuts

  • Suggest common phrases and Learn phrases from my typing: each can be turned off.
  • Phrases in the bar: "At the start" (the default) or "At the end", after the suggested words. Text shortcuts and corrections of common mistakes always come first.
  • Phrases you added: add, edit and delete, import and export.
  • Learned phrases: all of them, to move to your phrases or delete.
  • Deleted built-in phrases: the ones you hid from the bar, restorable here.

Text shortcuts

  • Add a shortcut: the code (such as «ع1») and the text it puts in.
  • Edit or delete any shortcut in the list, and move them all to another PC with import and export.

16. Long texts in Settings

  • Open the long texts list: the shortcut; click it to change it.
  • Add a long text: a name (up to 60 characters) and the text (up to 4,000 characters), with "Insert variable…" for the customer's name, today's date and more (see "Long texts: variables"). In the text box, Enter starts a new line and Ctrl + Enter saves.
  • Edit and delete; import and export to share them with colleagues.
  • Order: drag a text's card with the mouse to its new place; the shortcut's list shows the texts in the same order.

17. Updates and About

Updates: at start-up Itmam asks Microsoft Store whether a newer version exists (can be turned off), and "Check" asks now. The update itself comes through the Store. "Rate and review" opens the Store's window to give Itmam stars and write a review.

About: the version, the dictionary and your word counts, the Itmam website, "Rate and review", contacting the developer, the source code, the dictionary sources, and the privacy policy.

18. Privacy

  • Sends no data: no statistics, no crash reports. Its only automatic connection is asking Microsoft Store about updates, and it can be turned off. "Rate and review" opens the Store's window only when you press it.
  • Your text is not saved: only the words and short phrases you repeat, with how many times you used them, on your PC.
  • Silent where it should be: nothing is read from password fields, excluded apps, or apps running as administrator.
  • Open source: anyone can review the code on GitHub.

Full privacy policy: https://nasmn77.github.io/Itmam/en/privacy.html

19. Troubleshooting

The bar does not appear

  • Make sure the keyboard layout's language is turned on in Settings → "Languages".
  • Make sure Itmam is on: its icon is next to the clock, and its menu says "Turn off Itmam", not "Turn on Itmam".
  • Is the app excluded? Running as administrator? Full screen?
  • The bar appears after the number of letters set in General (two by default).

The bar is not at the caret

Some apps do not tell Windows where the caret is, so Itmam places the bar at the text field or at the mouse. Typing and inserting still work.

An odd word keeps being suggested

Right-click it, then "Delete" if it was learned, or "Block" so it is never suggested.

Moving Itmam to a new PC

General → "Back up…" on the old PC, then "Restore…" on the new one.
