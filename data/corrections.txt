# Common Arabic spelling mistakes that the dictionary cannot fix by itself (letters added, missing or
# words written together). Compiled by hand for Itmam; released under the MIT license like the code.
# Format: wrong = right | another right. When the wrong form is also a real word, the real word is
# listed too, so it stays one Tab away. Hamza seats and ة/ه, ى/ي at the end need no entry: the
# dictionary already puts the right spelling first. Lines starting with # are comments.
